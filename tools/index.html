Beyond the Room

Interactive Tools

Extend your care beyond the session

Free, thoughtfully designed tools for inner exploration and emotional well-being, made to carry the work of therapy into everyday life.

Self-guided tools grounded in psychotherapy and informed by clinical practice. Built with the help of AI, though no AI ever reads your answers. Always free, and always evolving.

Meant to support reflection and complement, not replace, professional mental health care.

Values

Values Clarification Exercise

For students, career starters, and anyone feeling stuck about what's next. Tap through what matters most to you, rank your top values, and see which kinds of work might fit, tailored to where you are in life. Mostly tapping, about 10 to 15 minutes.

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT) · Work Values
Try the Tool →
🧶
Thoughts & Habits

Untangle the Pattern

For when a rigid thought holds you back: putting off work you fear won't be good enough, or skipping events because you're sure you won't measure up. Map the pattern, spot the thinking trap, find a more balanced thought, and test it with one small step. About 10 minutes.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) · Behavioral Experiments
Try the Tool →
🌱
Nourishment

Daily Nourishment Check-In

For the days you need a little care. Notice what you need, receive a message that reminds you you're already enough, then choose words to carry or a small plan for today. Save it as a photo card. About 3 minutes.

ACT · Mindfulness · Meaning-Centered (Frankl)
Try the Tool →
🫧
Emotions & Body

Meet the Feeling

For when a feeling is big, confusing, or hard to name. Find the right word, notice where it lives in your body, ease that place with a guided exercise you can see and hear, then let it pass in its own time. About 5 to 8 minutes.

RAIN · Emotion Labeling · Mindful Grounding
Try the Tool →
🪞
Identity

Parts of Me

For the inner critic, the people pleaser, or the part that shuts down. Get to know what one protective part does, what it fears, and what it needs, then hear a message you can say to it. About 10 minutes.

Internal Family Systems (IFS)-Informed · Self-Compassion
Try the Tool →
🌊
OCD, Panic & Urges

Ride the Wave

For OCD, panic, and urges you feel you must act on: checking, washing, escaping, asking for reassurance. Map the cycle, then practice letting the urge rise and fall without acting on it, and watch your own wave pass. About 10 minutes.

Exposure & Response Prevention (ERP) · CBT for Panic
Try the Tool →
🌱
Low Mood & Burnout

Small Steps

When mood is low, we do less, and doing less lowers mood. Pick one to three tiny, doable steps for today, matched to your energy, then save them as a photo or a daily reminder. You don't have to feel motivated first. About 3 minutes.

Behavioral Activation
Try the Tool →

Boundaries & People-Pleasing

Kind Boundaries

For when it's hard to say no, ask for what you need, or set a limit without guilt. Build a clear, kind message, get ready for pushback, and leave with words you can actually say. About 7 minutes.

DBT Interpersonal Effectiveness (DEAR MAN)

Couples & Communication

Gentle Start-Up

How a hard conversation starts often shapes how it ends. Turn a complaint into "I feel… about… I need…", check for the Four Horsemen, and keep a few repair phrases ready. About 7 minutes.

Gottman Method: Gentle Start-Up & Repair

Identity & Life Transitions

Life Chapters

Name the chapters of your life so far, notice the thread that has carried you through, gently rewrite an old story about yourself, and title the chapter you're beginning. About 8 minutes.

Narrative Therapy

Hope & Future Self

Best Possible Self

Imagine your life ahead, going as well as it reasonably could. Receive a letter from your future self, and choose one small step toward it. About 7 minutes.

Positive Psychology: Best Possible Self

Avoidance & Procrastination

Drop the Rope

For the things you keep putting off, and the feelings you'd rather not feel. See why what you resist persists, practice making room for the discomfort, and take one small, willing step. About 6 minutes.

ACT: Willingness & Acceptance

Family & Culture

Roots & Messages

Draw a simple, visual map of your family across three generations. Notice the messages passed down to you, and choose which ones to carry, rewrite, or leave behind. About 12 minutes.

Genogram & Family Systems

Inner Critic & Shame

Self-Compassion Break

For when you're being hard on yourself. Notice the hurt, remember you're not alone, and offer yourself the kindness you'd give a friend. Ends with three phrases in your own words, saved as a card for hard moments. About 4 minutes.

Self-Compassion (Neff) · Compassion-Focused

Anxiety & Worry

Worry Time

For worry that follows you all day. Park each worry in a jar as it comes up, then return at a set time to see what faded and sort what's left: one next step, or let it float by. Your jar saves on this device. 30 seconds to park, about 15 minutes for worry time.

CBT Worry Postponement · ACT Defusion
