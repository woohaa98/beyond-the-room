Worry Time

Worries can follow you all day. Instead of fighting them or giving in, give them an appointment. Park each worry in your jar as it comes up, then come back at a set time to sort through what's left. Many will have faded by then. 30 seconds to park a worry, about 15 minutes for worry time.

Based on worry postponement (stimulus control for worry), a cognitive behavioral technique studied for generalized anxiety, with a leaves-on-a-stream exercise from Acceptance and Commitment Therapy. A self-guided practice, not a substitute for care.
