How it works

Three steps. No setup.

No accounts, no portal, nothing to install, for you or your client.

STEP 1

Pick a tool

Choose one that fits what your client is working on, like riding out panic, untangling a thought, or mapping family messages.

STEP 2

Send one link

Add your name and it opens as "Shared by your therapist." Your client works through it at home, on their own device, in a few minutes.

STEP 3

They bring it back

At the end, your client chooses what to share: an emailed summary or a PDF for your next session. You start where their week left off.

Practice plans

A purposeful plan, not an open chat.

Many people already pour their thoughts and feelings into general-purpose chatbots. Open chat goes wherever the conversation drifts. Beyond the Room follows a plan: the therapist introduces a defined set of tools, for a specific concern, for a set amount of time, and then reviews it together with the client.

Open-ended chat

  • ScopeAny topic, wherever it drifts
  • Who shapes itThe model and the moment
  • TimeframeOpen-ended
  • DataA conversation log in the cloud

A practice plan

  • ScopeOne concern, one clear purpose
  • Who shapes itThe therapist, for this client
  • TimeframeA week, a month, until next session
  • DataStays on the client's device; shared by choice

Every plan has a purpose, and purposes can be combined:

  • Learn · understand what's happening
  • Practice · build a skill
  • Notice · gather information, like triggers or moods
  • Cope · get through hard moments

Questions

Common questions.

Is it free? Will it stay free?

Yes. The tools and worksheets are free to use with your clients, and Beyond the Room is built as an open commons so they stay that way.

How is this different from other therapy tools?

AI therapy apps put a chatbot in front of your client; worksheet libraries and homework platforms usually mean static sheets, paid tiers, or client data stored on their servers. Beyond the Room keeps you at the center, is free and open for therapists to adapt and share, and keeps your client's answers on their own device. See the full comparison.

Do my clients need an account or an app?

No. Each tool opens in any web browser on a phone or computer. There's nothing to download and no sign-up.

Where does my client's information go?

Nowhere. Answers stay in the client's own browser, and some tools save progress there so they can come back. A summary leaves the device only if your client chooses to email, print, or copy it. They can erase everything with one tap.

How does the summary reach me?

When you send a link with your name and work email, the summary opens in your client's own email app, addressed to you, for them to review and send. Or they can save a PDF and bring it to session. Regular email isn't fully private, and the tools tell clients so.

Is this therapy, or crisis support?

No. The tools support reflection and skill practice between sessions, alongside your care. They aren't a substitute for therapy and aren't for emergencies. Tools that touch distress point to 988.

Can I adapt a tool, or build my own with AI?

That's where this is headed. Each tool is a single file, so a therapist can adapt it with an AI assistant. A remix kit with shared privacy and safety rules is coming, and versions shared back to the library are peer-reviewed by clinicians. Today you can send feedback on any tool, and pilot therapists help shape what comes next.
